# Source and license

## Ground magnetometer data

IMAGE magnetometer network, Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI) and
partner institutes (TGO, SGU, DTU, IGF, and others).

Bulk data service: https://space.fmi.fi/image/www/data_download.php
(format=text, sample_rate=60, monthly chunks).

IMAGE rules of the road (https://space.fmi.fi/image/data.html): the data are
free for scientific use; users are requested to acknowledge the IMAGE network
and the institutes operating the stations. This challenge dataset acknowledges
the IMAGE magnetometer network maintained by FMI and partners.

## Solar wind / IMF driver data

NASA/GSFC Space Physics Data Facility, OMNI high-resolution (1-minute) data set
(omni_min monthly files), https://spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/data/omni/high_res_omni/
US Government work; please cite King & Papitashvili (OMNI) in publications.

## This derived dataset

The derived, anonymized challenge dataset (windowed anomalies, case structure,
labels) is dedicated to the public domain under CC0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), with the IMAGE
attribution above retained as required by the source's rules of the road.
